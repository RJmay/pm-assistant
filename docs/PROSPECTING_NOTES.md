*Companion to `docs/prospects.csv` (the pipeline tracker) and `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`

## The 30-second pitch (memorise — phone, doorway, anywhere)

> "I've built a tool for Queensland property managers. It reads every inbound
> email — tenants, owners, tradies — and writes the reply for you: compliant
> with the new tenancy rules, in your agency's voice. Your PM just reviews and
> sends; it never sends anything on its own. PMs get back an hour or two a
> day, which means more doors without hiring. Can I show you a 10-minute live
> demo this week?"

## Cold-call script (after you've emailed)

1. **Open:** "Hi, it's Ryan May — I emailed a couple of days ago about an AI
   tool that drafts tenant email replies for QLD property managers. I know
   email's easy to miss, so I thought I'd just call. Have you got 30 seconds?"
2. **Pitch:** the 30-second pitch above.
3. **Ask:** "Could I show you a 10-minute live demo this week? I'm local —
   I can come to you, or do it over a video call."
4. **If gatekeeper:** "Who looks after the property management side — the
   principal or a department head? When's a good time to catch them?"
   Get a NAME and a TIME. That's a successful call.
5. **If no:** "No worries — can I leave you a one-pager in case the inbox
   ever gets out of hand?" (Always end friendly; this market talks.)

**Calling window:** Tue–Thu, 9:30–11:30am. Never Monday morning, never
Friday afternoon.

## Cold email + follow-ups (3 touches max)

- **Touch 1 (email):** the template in the session notes — subject
  "Helping [Agency] PMs handle more doors — 10-min demo?", under 150 words,
  no attachments, no links.
- **Touch 2 (call, day 2–3):** script above.
- **Touch 3 (email, day 5–7):** "Hi [Name] — just floating this back up.
  Happy to do the 10-minute demo whenever suits, even over a quick video
  call. Cheers, Ryan."
- Then stop. Mark the outcome and move on — re-approach in a quarter.

## Objection cards (one-liners)

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "What if the AI gets the law wrong?" | "It can't send anything — ever. A human approves every email, and the legal numbers come from a versioned QLD rules engine with citations, not the AI. When it isn't sure, it refuses and says why." |
| "We use PropertyMe / Console." | "Keep it — this isn't trust accounting. It sits on your inbox and works alongside it. We import your rent roll from a PropertyMe export in setup." |
| "AI will upset our tenants." | "Tenants still get a human — your PM — just in minutes instead of days, in your voice." |
| "How much?" | "Per door per month — single-digit dollars, a fraction of the PM hours it returns. But the pilot's free: two weeks, a slice of your rent roll, measure the time saved, walk away if it's not obvious." |
| "We're too busy to set anything up." | "That's exactly the point. Setup is on me — half an hour, I connect the inbox and import the rent roll. Your PMs just start reviewing drafts." |
| "Replace my staff?" | "Amplifies them. One PM handles more doors; nobody loses a job — you stop needing the next hire as the book grows." |

## How to read the tracker (`prospects.csv`)

- **tier** — A = boutique/PM-focused (owner answers the phone, fastest to
  close); B = franchise office (find the PM department head); C = needs more
  digging.
- **email_verified** — `yes` means the address was re-checked as literally
  published on the cited page on research day. `no email` = phone-first
  prospect (general inboxes get gatekeepered anyway).
- **touch columns** — date + outcome per touch ("e" email / "c" call /
  "w" walk-in). Three touches, then `status` → `parked` or `meeting`.
- The angle column is your first sentence after "Have you got 30 seconds?" —
  use their own claim ("you advertise 24/7 maintenance…") to make it about them.

## Ground rules

- Real published contacts only — never scraped-and-guessed addresses (bounces
  poison your Gmail's sender reputation).
- Spam Act 2003: identify yourself, business-relevant outreach, honour any
  "not interested" immediately. One-to-one personalised email is fine;
  mass-blasting the same text to 30 inboxes in one morning is not — space it.
- Send from your real account; demo from the demo tenant; never show a real
  client's data to another agency.
